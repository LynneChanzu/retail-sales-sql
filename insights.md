## RETAIL SALES PERFORMANCE REPORT
Reporting period : January 2025 – September 2026

Data source: Retail Sales & Customer Information dataset
### Introduction
This report analyses retail sales and customer purchasing patterns to evaluate business performance, identify key revenue and profit drivers, and highlight opportunities for improvement.
### 1. Overall Revenue and Profitability
The business generated a total revenue of **KSh 24,155,371.70** against a total cost of **KSh 21,264,450.00**, resulting in a gross profit of KSh **2,890,921.70** and a profit margin of **11.97%**.
Although the business is profitable, its 11.97% profit margin indicates that a significant proportion of revenue is absorbed by product costs, leaving limited room for additional operating expenses.
#### Recommendation
Review supplier pricing, product sourcing costs and discount policies to identify opportunities to reduce costs and improve profitability.

### 2. Most Profitable Product Category
Mobile Phones emerged as the most profitable category, generating **KSh 6,942,060.65** in revenue and **KSh 728,760.65** in profit, with a profit margin of approximately **10.50%**.
The category makes the largest contribution to overall profit, making it an important driver of business performance. However, its margin is below the overall business margin of 11.97%.
#### Recommendation
Maintain product availability while reviewing supplier costs and discount policies to identify opportunities to improve the category's profit margin.
